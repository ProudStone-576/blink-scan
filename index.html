Intended use

Blink Scan lets a person who cannot speak or type choose words and phrases with deliberate eye blinks. A carer sets it up and stays within hearing.

Limits

It is not a certified medical device and not an emergency call system. Keep another way to call for help. Glasses glare, low light and eyelid conditions change how well blinks are read, so calibrate again when the position or the light changes.

Privacy

The camera image is read on this device only. Nothing is recorded or sent. Phrases, settings and the records below stay in this browser.
